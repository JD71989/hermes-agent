# Guardian OS Architecture

## Locked operating model

- **Guardian Core orchestrates everything.**
- **Departments provide capabilities.**
- **Agents perform capabilities.**
- **Plugins combine capabilities into businesses.**
- **Plugin Factory turns approved ideas into executable business specifications.**

## Lifecycle

`IDEA -> VALIDATE -> BUSINESS MODEL -> CAPABILITY MAP -> PLUGIN SPEC -> BUILD -> TEST -> DEPLOY -> OPERATE -> MEASURE -> LEARN -> SCALE`

Business engines are additive modules. They do not become new departments and they do not duplicate core capabilities.

## Safety boundaries

Plugins declare required departments, agents, capabilities, revenue models, credentials, and risk controls. External actions remain disabled until their explicit integration and approval gates are satisfied. Live financial execution remains locked.

## Initial departments

Marketing; Security; Research & Intelligence; Engineering; Sales & Leads; Finance; Operations.

## Initial capability restoration

Telephone agents are restored as an Operations capability that business plugins can declare as a dependency.

## First business plugin

News Media Engine is the first business-engine manifest. Its complete production pipeline is still a build target; the manifest is the system-of-record for its architecture rather than a claim that every connector is live.
